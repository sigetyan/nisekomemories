  1. We decide whether the session goes ahead, based on forecasts, local conditions and everyone's safety.   3. If we decide it is not safe to shoot, for example during storm or heavy snow warnings, when roads are closed or when ski lifts are not running, we will move your session to another time or day during your stay at no charge.

Instead of credit, you can ask for a refund of what you paid less a ¥5,000 handling fee, for any reason (for example, because you are returning home), by telling us within 30 days of our notice that the session cannot be moved. Except in that case, credit cannot be exchanged for cash. When we tell you the session cannot be moved, our email will explain that you can choose credit or a refund, and the deadline for asking for a refund.
  5. If the weather turns during a session and we stop it for safety, we deliver the photos taken. If the photographer is free during your stay, we shoot the remaining time at no charge. If that is not possible, we refund part of the price in proportion to the time not shot.
