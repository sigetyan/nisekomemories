1. Scope

  1. These Terms apply to the photography agreement (the "Agreement") between us and the person who makes the booking ("you").
  2. Please share these Terms with everyone taking part in the session.
  3. If you are under 18, please book with the consent of a parent or guardian.
  4. If we confirm specific conditions with you in writing when you book, those conditions take priority over these Terms.

2. Booking and when the Agreement starts

  1. Tell us your preferred date and plan through the enquiry form or by email. We check our photographer's availability and, if the date is open, send you a booking page.
  2. The Agreement is formed once you complete the booking page, the deposit is paid, and we send you a booking confirmation email.
  3. Your date is not held until we receive the deposit.

3. What each plan includes

  1. Session length, group size, number of locations and the approximate number of edited images for each plan are shown on our packages. Image numbers are a guide and can vary with conditions.
  2. We shoot in the Niseko area (including Niseko Town, Kutchan and the Konbu Onsen area of Rankoshi), Otaru and the Lake Toya area. Photographer travel to these areas is included in the price.
  3. Other locations are possible only by prior agreement, with a travel fee quoted in advance.
  4. If your group is larger than the plan allows, please ask us before booking.
  5. Your own transport to the location, and any entry or facility fees, are your responsibility.

4. Prices and payment

  1. All prices include Japanese consumption tax.
  2. A deposit of 50% of the price is paid when you book. The balance is due on the day of the session. You can pay by credit card or bank transfer.
  3. Bank transfer fees are paid by you.
  4. The price does not change if fewer people attend or if you choose to finish early.

6. Date changes

  1. Once a booking is confirmed, the date cannot be changed. If you would like a different date, the original booking is cancelled under clause 5 and a new booking is made, including a new deposit.
  2. If something serious and outside your control happens, such as a sudden life-threatening illness or injury or a natural disaster, we will talk with you about moving the date on a case by case basis. We may ask for supporting documents.
  3. Adjusting the start time on the same day is possible if the photographer's schedule allows.

7. Weather

  1. We decide whether the session goes ahead, based on forecasts, local conditions and everyone's safety. We will normally let you know by 6pm (Japan time) the day before, or before the start time on the day.
  2. Light rain, snow, cloud and fog do not stop a session as long as we can shoot safely. Hokkaido weather often makes for memorable photos.
  3. If we decide it is not safe to shoot, for example during storm or heavy snow warnings, or when roads or ski lifts are closed, we will move your session to another time or day during your stay at no charge.
  4. If no other time works during your stay, everything you have paid is kept as a credit, valid for 12 months from the original session date and usable for any plan. Any price difference is settled when you use it.
  5. If the weather turns during a session and we have to stop, the session counts as complete if at least half of the booked time was shot. If less than half was shot, the session is moved or credited as above.

8. Cancellation by us

  1. If we cannot shoot because of our own circumstances, such as the photographer's sudden illness or injury, we will offer another photographer from our team or another date during your stay.
  2. If you do not accept either option, or we cannot arrange one, we refund everything you have paid in full.
  3. If we start late, we extend the session to give you the full booked time. If that is not possible, we refund the price in proportion to the time lost.

9. On the day

  1. We will confirm the meeting point and time with you by email beforehand.
  2. If you arrive late, the session still ends at the scheduled time and no refund is given.
  3. Extending a session is possible only if the photographer's schedule allows, at a fee we confirm with you before extending.
  4. We take your requests into account, but composition, locations, and the selection and editing of images are at the photographer's discretion.
  5. We will not shoot anywhere unsafe, or anywhere that breaks the law or a facility's rules.

10. Niseko Ski Photography plan

  1. This plan runs in winter only, with a professional ski guide. The guide and photographer choose runs and locations based on snow, weather and crowds.
  2. Lift passes are not included; please bring your own.
  3. Participants must be able to ski or snowboard intermediate runs safely. If resort rules or the guide's instructions are not followed, we may stop the session for safety, without a refund.
  4. Sessions take place inside the resort's managed area only. We do not shoot in the backcountry.
  5. We recommend wearing a helmet and having insurance that covers skiing injuries.
  6. If lifts close, for example because of strong wind, clause 7 applies.

11. Delivery

  1. We deliver a selection of edited images to your private online gallery within 5 business days of the day after your session. Business days exclude weekends, Japanese public holidays and 29 December to 3 January.
  2. Images are delivered as high resolution JPEG files. Unedited (RAW) files are not provided.
  3. Standard colour and exposure editing is included. Body shaping, removing people and other retouching beyond standard editing are not offered.
  4. The gallery has no set expiry date, but please download your images soon after delivery. If we ever need to close galleries, we will give at least 30 days' notice.
  5. We keep session files for a period after delivery, but cannot guarantee re-delivery after that period ends.

13. Use of photos by us

  3. We are happy to follow other preferences too, for example only showing photos where your children's faces cannot be seen.
  4. We never publish your name or other personal details without your consent.

  5. Please tell us promptly if any booking details change, such as group size or contact details.
