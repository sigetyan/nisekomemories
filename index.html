Who we are

Niseko's full-time
portrait team.


Niseko Memories is a professional portrait photography service based in Niseko, Hokkaido, operated by Nomado Ltd. We offer vacation and family photography packages from ¥50,000, covering Niseko, Otaru, and Lake Toya across all four seasons.

We guide people through Hokkaido's landscapes at a pace that lets you enjoy it. Sessions are relaxed and built around you rather than a shot list. We know the light across every season, from deep winter powder to summer lupins and autumn foliage.

We work under Nomado Ltd, the same team behind Nomad Weddings Japan, Hokkaido's dedicated elopement and wedding photography service. You get that same standard of care and attention for your vacation.

Common questions

Answered
honestly.

How much does a Niseko photoshoot cost?

Niseko Memories charges ¥50,000 for a one-hour session for couples or families of up to 5. The Tour Groups package is ¥80,000 for 2 hours at 2 locations for groups up to 30. The Niseko Ski Photography package is ¥110,000 and includes a professional ski guide. All prices include editing and online gallery delivery within 5 working days.

How many photos will we receive?

A 1-hour session typically produces 50 to 80 professionally edited images. A 2-hour session produces around 120 to 160. All images are delivered in full resolution via a private online gallery within 5 working days, downloadable to any device.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Hokkaido weather is part of what makes it special. Snow, mist, and overcast winter skies often produce the best images. For sessions significantly affected by conditions, the team works with you to adapt timing or reschedule where possible. We know the weather windows here better than anyone.

Can you photograph us on the mountain?

Yes. The Niseko Ski Photography package is designed exactly for this. A professional ski guide accompanies the session to access the best terrain safely. Powder runs, lift shots, and candid ski moments. Available winter season only.

Do you photograph in summer and autumn?

Yes. Hokkaido photographs well in every season. Lupins bloom in late May and June, lavender in July and August, foliage turns crimson in October. We can advise on the precise timing for each season to help you plan around your travel dates.

Where do you photograph in Hokkaido?

Niseko Memories is based in Niseko and covers Niseko, Hirafu, Hanazono, Otaru, and Lake Toya. For wedding photography anywhere in Hokkaido, please visit Nomad Weddings Japan.
