Who we are

Niseko's full-time
portrait team.


We are a team of professional portrait photographers based in Niseko, Hokkaido. Our passion is guiding people on a journey through one of Japan's most remarkable landscapes, crafting comfortable, fun sessions that capture the essence of who you are together.

Unlike visiting photographers, we are here year-round. We know the light across every season, from deep winter powder to summer lupins and autumn foliage. That local knowledge shows in every frame we deliver.

We work under Nomado Ltd, the same team behind Nomad Weddings Japan — Hokkaido's dedicated elopement and wedding photography service. You get that same standard of care, attention, and photography for your vacation.

Common questions

Answered
honestly.

How much does a Niseko photoshoot cost?

Packages start from ¥50,000 for a one-hour session for couples or families of up to 5. The Tour Groups package is ¥80,000 for 2 hours at 2 locations for groups up to 30. The Niseko Ski Photography package is ¥110,000 and includes a professional ski guide. All prices include editing and online gallery delivery.

How many photos will we receive?

A 1-hour session typically produces 50 to 80 professionally edited images. A 2-hour session produces around 120 to 160. All images are delivered in full resolution via a private online gallery within 5 working days. You can download directly to any device.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Hokkaido weather is part of what makes it special. Snow, mist, and overcast winter skies often produce our best images. For sessions significantly affected by conditions, we work with you to adapt timing or reschedule where possible. We know the weather windows here better than anyone.

Can you photograph us on the mountain?

Yes. Our Niseko Ski Photography package is designed exactly for this. A professional ski guide accompanies the session to access the best terrain safely. We capture powder runs, lift shots, and candid ski moments. Available winter season only.

Do you photograph in summer and autumn?

Yes, and Hokkaido is extraordinary year-round. Lupins bloom in late May and June, lavender in July and August, foliage turns crimson in October. We know the precise timing for each and will help you plan around your travel dates.

Where do you photograph in Hokkaido?

We are based in Niseko and cover Niseko, Hirafu, Hanazono, Otaru, Lake Toya, and Sapporo. We do not currently cover Furano, Biei, Sapporo beyond the city centre, or Shiretoko. For wedding photography, please visit Nomad Weddings Japan.
